Live layerThe marks working together over the view. Hover reads a cell, click pins up to three notes, two pins measure. Click a pin to remove it.

PinClick to drop a numbered dot with an elbow leader and a two-line readout. Up to three; the oldest drops off.
MeasureConsecutive pins are joined by a dashed line with the distance in mm.
